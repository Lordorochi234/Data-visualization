Visual Analysis

Key Insights

  • Accident claims dominate: Own damage and third-party accidents account for 63.7% of all claims, highlighting road safety as a critical concern.
  • Nairobi concentration: 44% of claims originate from Nairobi, reflecting higher vehicle density and traffic congestion.
  • Theft remains significant: Vehicle theft claims average KES 300,000, the highest average among claim types.
  • Comprehensive policies at higher risk: 16.5% claims ratio vs. 1.4% for third-party only, justifying premium differences.
  • Seasonal patterns: Weather-related claims (6.4%) spike during rainy seasons (April-May, October-November).

R Analysis Methodology

Tools Used: R (version 4.3), tidyverse, ggplot2, dplyr

Data Processing: Claims data cleaned and categorized by type, region, and insurance class. Outliers removed using IQR method.

Statistical Methods: Descriptive statistics, frequency distributions, and percentage calculations.

Visualization: Created using ggplot2 for R analysis, Chart.js for web presentation.
